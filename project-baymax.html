13 / COMBAT ROBOTICS

BAYMAX

A combat robotics platform designed and built for high-performance arena competition, maneuverability and impact resistance.

COMPLETED
COMBAT ROBOTICS BLDC MOTOR CONTROL MECHANICAL DESIGN
01 / OVERVIEW

Built to fight. Designed to survive.

BAYMAX is a combat robotics platform developed as part of a competitive robotics system.

The project combines mechanical fabrication, high-power motor control, electronics integration and radio-controlled operation into a compact combat-ready platform.

02 / DESIGN

Engineering priorities.

Mechanical Design

  • Rigid and compact chassis construction.
  • Protection of critical electronics.
  • Low center of gravity.
  • Structural durability during impacts.

Combat Performance

  • High torque drive system.
  • Rapid directional control.
  • Reliable radio control.
  • Optimized weight distribution.
03 / BUILD

From CAD to combat.

BAYMAX was developed through a combination of mechanical design, fabrication, electronics integration and repeated testing.

04 / HARDWARE

System architecture.

Drive System High-power electric drive system
Motors BLDC motor-based propulsion
Control Radio-controlled drive and steering
Chassis Custom fabricated combat chassis
Power High-current battery power system
05 / TESTING

Tested for the arena.

Testing focused on drive response, maneuverability, structural integrity, electronics reliability and overall system performance.

06 / RESULT

A completed combat platform.

BAYMAX represents a complete integration of mechanical design, fabrication, electronics, motor control and radio operation into a functional combat robot.

The project provided practical experience in designing systems where mechanical strength, electrical power and control performance must work together under demanding conditions.
