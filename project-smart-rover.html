01 / OVERVIEW

A mobile platform built to evolve.

The Smart Rover is an ongoing mobile robotics project designed as a flexible platform for experimenting with robotic movement, sensing, control and autonomous behaviour.

Rather than building the robot around a single fixed task, the platform is being developed with future upgrades and different robotic behaviours in mind.

The project brings together mechanical design, embedded electronics and robotics software into one ground-based system.

02 / OBJECTIVES

Built around experimentation.

Mobility

06 / DEVELOPMENT

Iterating toward autonomous behaviour.

Development is being carried out incrementally, starting with the mechanical platform and basic movement before progressively integrating more sensing and intelligent behaviour.

This approach makes it possible to validate each subsystem independently before combining them into a complete robotic system.

07 / CONTROL

Feedback-driven robotics.

The long-term direction of the rover is to move beyond simple remote-controlled motion and toward behaviour based on sensor feedback.

This creates opportunities for navigation, obstacle detection, autonomous movement and higher-level robotic decision making.

09 / FUTURE

The platform is just the beginning.

The Smart Rover is intended to become a reusable robotics platform for testing different sensors, control algorithms, navigation systems and autonomous behaviours.

Its modular nature allows new capabilities to be added without redesigning the entire robot.
