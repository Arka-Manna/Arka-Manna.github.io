PRISMATIC

A modular robotic platform developed for inspection, surveillance, monitoring and automated tasks.

IN DEVELOPMENT

01 / OVERVIEW

Building a platform, not just a robot.

PRISMATIC is a modular robotic platform developed to explore robotic inspection, surveillance, monitoring and automated operations.

The project combines mechanical design, embedded electronics, sensing, wireless communication and robotic control into a single integrated platform.

02 / SYSTEM

Design objectives.

Primary Objectives

  • Develop a modular robotic platform.
  • Integrate multiple sensors and actuators.
  • Enable remote robotic operation.
  • Explore autonomous robotic functions.

Engineering Focus

  • Mechanical structure and integration.
  • Embedded control systems.
  • Wireless communication.
  • Sensor integration and data processing.
03 / DEVELOPMENT

From concept to prototype.

The development process involves iterative mechanical construction, electronics integration, firmware development and testing.
