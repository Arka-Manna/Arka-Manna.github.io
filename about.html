I'm Arka Manna.

I'm a passionate Mechanical Engineering student with a deep interest in robotics, UAVs, embedded systems, and mechanical design.

I enjoy taking an idea from a concept and turning it into something that actually works. My projects combine mechanical design, electronics, programming, fabrication, and experimentation.

From autonomous robots and ESP32-based systems to RC aircraft and UAVs, I love exploring technology by building things from scratch.

For me, engineering isn't just about knowing how something works. It's about asking, "What can I build with it?"
